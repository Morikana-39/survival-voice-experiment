# Participant-facing revision — 2026-09-08

Protocol: `survival-voice-v1.3-warmup`. Supersedes v1.2 for new sessions; old records are preserved and old previews must restart. The fixed 12-item permutation and conformity formulas are unchanged.

## Implemented

- Eligibility is whole-number age **12–65 inclusive**, checked in the form and server.
- Participant-facing title, consent, instructions, scenario, ranking/discussion labels, questionnaire and debrief use the researcher's revised Chinese wording. The five seven-point items remain required, with midpoint label “一般”. SP5 now describes humanized expression rather than human sensitivity; this is a substantive adaptation and needs translation/construct review before recruitment. Historical responses must not be pooled without accounting for protocol/wording version.
- Confirmed withdrawal clears server-side responses and demographics and keeps the participation-code/session lock. A second confirmation is required throughout active participation. Before consent/session creation, declining simply leaves the form without creating a research record; it cannot permanently identify/block someone across devices without additional identifiers or consent.
- The requested phrase “匿名参与状态信息” is present in the consent draft. The implementation retains hashed invitation/session identifiers and participation state; this should not be represented as a guarantee of irreversible anonymization. The researcher's privacy review must account for any recruitment linkage and provider retention/backups. App-level deletion does not assert deletion from every infrastructure backup.
- Back navigation opens read-only consent/instruction/scenario review. Demographics can return to consent before session creation. Review does not rewind server phases or unmount the current unfinished form; completed sound checks, rankings and questionnaires cannot be reopened. No informational Back control appears during discussion, including warm-up.
- The scenario is shown in both ranking stages. Final ranking is one editable list initialized from the initial ranking, with a smaller AI suggestion list to its left (stacked left-aligned on very narrow screens). “重置” restores the initial order and logs reset movements without changing the saved initial ranking.
- Consecutive item clicks swap positions; drag insertion and adjacent up/down controls retain their normal behavior. Whole-card native drag images replace the small handle image. Touch dragging from the rank handle has a whole-card visual overlay. Layout movements animate with a reduced-motion alternative.
- The unchanged supplied WarmUpAudio.mp3 is used as a common introduction for both conditions. Verified mono MP3, 48 kHz, 13.632 seconds. Warm-up is server-tracked separately from the 12 item exposures and must finish before any reason. Interrupted playback requires replay. This is a technical check, not a content/listening review.
- Playback completion directly enables Continue; the heard-audio checkbox and playback progress bar are removed. Playback-time, mute, visibility and seeking checks remain. Hardware/tab mute still cannot be reliably detected.
- Condition is returned to the participant interface only at completion. Preview debrief explicitly describes fluent audition rather than claiming random study assignment.
- “重置实验（仅预览）” is available throughout preview with confirmation. It creates a fresh preview after consent/demographics, does not alter an existing study session, and should be removed before the final research release.

## Launch boundary

This remains a researcher preview. The 12 disfluent recordings, matched-pair review, actual guardian-permission process, ethics/privacy approval and study contact/retention details remain outstanding. No collection-readiness flags are enabled and no paid services are configured. Technical checks do not substitute for a target-device interaction/listening pilot.
